## Message standard

Clothing and gear requests ask whether the company offers artist pricing, store credit, product support, loaner gear, or a creator partnership; they do not promise a backlink or endorsement. Festival requests ask for a paid 5–8 song set plus round-trip Hawaii travel, hotel, and local ground transport for a mainland date. Festival EPK: https://www.elionmusic.com/downloads/festival-epk/ .
